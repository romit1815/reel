How to download Facebook Reels

Three easy steps to save public Facebook reels to your device.

Step 1: Copy the Facebook link

Open Facebook, find the public reel, tap Share, and copy the URL.

Step 2: Paste it here

Paste the copied URL into the downloader input box above and press the Download button.

Step 3: Save the file

Choose an available HD or SD option. Your browser downloads the file to its usual location.

In this guide: copy a Reel link · check public access · choose a quality · troubleshoot a link · use downloads responsibly · FAQs

Use the Reel link, not the profile link

This downloader needs the address of one specific Reel. A creator’s profile address points to their page, so it does not tell the tool which video to load. In Facebook, open the Reel itself, choose Share, then Copy Link.

Reel links often look like facebook.com/reel/…. Facebook may also create a short fb.watch/… link. Paste the full link, including https://, into the box above.

If Facebook gives you a post or share link instead, try opening it in your browser first. When the Reel opens, copy the address shown in the browser bar.

Check that the Reel is public

Before troubleshooting the downloader, open the link in a private or incognito browser window where you are not signed in to Facebook. If the Reel plays there, it is more likely to be publicly reachable. If Facebook asks you to log in, says the content is unavailable, or limits it to friends or a group, FBviddl cannot access it.

This check is a useful guide, not a guarantee: Facebook can change availability, and a public Reel may still fail if its link has changed or the source is temporarily unavailable.

Copy and submit a Reel link

  1. Open the Reel in the Facebook app or website.
  2. Use Share → Copy Link, or copy the Reel’s address from your browser.
  3. Return to this page and paste the full link into the Reel field.
  4. Select Download and wait for the preview and available file options.

On a phone, you may need to switch from the Facebook app to your browser to use the downloader. You do not need to enter or share your Facebook password.

Choose a file that fits your needs

When the Reel is available, the page may offer HD and SD downloads. Choose HD when you want more detail and have enough storage or bandwidth. Choose SD when a smaller file is more useful. The page only shows options returned for that Reel.

These labels describe the available source versions; they do not promise a particular resolution or file size. The downloader cannot restore detail lost in the original upload or Facebook’s video processing.

Your browser manages the download. Look in its download list or the device’s Downloads folder if the file does not appear on screen.

Troubleshoot a Facebook Reel link

Use the symptom below to decide what to check first. Re-copying the link is useful when Facebook has changed or shortened the address.

What you see Likely cause What to try
The link is rejected The pasted text may be a profile name, incomplete link, or copied caption Open the Reel itself and copy its link again. Paste the full web address beginning with https://.
Processing finishes with an error The Reel may be private, deleted, region-limited, or unavailable to logged-out viewers Test the link in a private browser window. If it does not play there, FBviddl cannot retrieve it.
The page keeps processing The source or worker may be responding slowly Check your connection, copy a fresh link, and try once more. If it continues, try again later or contact us.
The preview appears but no quality option does No downloadable source was returned for that Reel Retry with the Reel’s direct link. If the options still do not appear, this source is not currently available.
You selected Download but cannot find the file The browser may have saved it without opening a new screen Open the browser’s downloads list and check the device’s Downloads folder. Confirm that downloads are allowed for the browser.
The file is blurry or will not play The source may be low quality or the download may have been interrupted Try the other available quality or download again. A low-quality source cannot be made sharper by downloading it.
